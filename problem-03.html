Practical Class Sequence

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Enter the laboratory with the required materials.
  3. Listen to the faculty explanation.
  4. Perform the assigned practical task.
  5. Save the work and submit it before leaving.

Classroom Rules

  • Attendance is mandatory according to university rules.
  • Bring the required notebook and study material.
  • Do not disturb other students during lectures.
  • Keep the classroom and laboratory clean.
  • Follow laboratory safety instructions.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform hands-on work.
Tutorial
A guided session used for practice, discussion and problem solving.
Internal Assessment
Assessment conducted during the academic term.
Practical
A task or examination used to test practical knowledge and skills.
