Admission Overview

Welcome to the admission handbook of GLA University, Greater Noida. This page gives students a simple overview of the admission process, eligibility, documents, fees and important terms.

Eligibility

Students should check the eligibility requirements for their selected programme before applying. Meeting the required eligibility criteria is mandatory.

Application deadline: Check the official admission portal for the current deadline.

Application Process

  1. Choose the programme you want to apply for.
  2. Register on the official admission portal.
  3. Fill in the application form carefully.
  4. Pay the required registration fee.
  5. Appear for the required admission test or selection process.
  6. Check the admission result.
  7. Complete document verification and secure your seat.

Required Documents

  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Identity proof
  • Class 10 marksheet
  • Class 12 marksheet
  • Transfer or migration certificate, if required
  • Other documents required for the selected programme

Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions a student must satisfy to apply for a programme.
Merit List
A list prepared according to the applicable admission criteria and student performance.
Counselling
A process in which students receive guidance about programmes, seats and admission choices.
Scholarship
Financial support offered to eligible students according to the applicable scholarship rules.
Registration
The process of submitting details to begin an admission application.
