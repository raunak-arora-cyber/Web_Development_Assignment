How to Choose Your Laptop

  2. Choose a processor suitable for your work.
  3. Check RAM and storage capacity.
  4. Compare display size and resolution.

Technical Terms

RAM
Memory used by the computer to temporarily hold data for active tasks.
SSD
Solid State Drive used for storing files and applications.
CPU
Central Processing Unit that performs the main processing work of a computer.
GPU
Graphics Processing Unit that handles graphics and visual processing.
Refresh Rate
The number of times a display refreshes its image per second, measured in Hz.
Battery Capacity
The amount of electrical energy a laptop battery can store.
