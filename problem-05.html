GLA University Presents: Udaan 2026

Annual College Fest 2026

Udaan 2026 is a three-day college festival celebrating creativity, technology, music, sports and teamwork.

Fest Introduction

The fest brings students together through competitions, performances, workshops and fun activities. Students can participate individually or as teams.

Registration Process

  1. Select the competition you want to join.
  2. Read the eligibility and event rules.
  3. Fill in the registration form.
  4. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  5. Save your registration/reference number.
  6. Reach the venue before the reporting time.

Registration Deadline: 5 October 2026

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college identity card.
  • Participants must report before the scheduled reporting time.
  • Follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Maintain discipline at all venues.
  • Do not damage college property.
  • Use respectful language with other participants and volunteers.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event open to participants who satisfy the stated eligibility rules.
Team Event
An event in which two or more participants compete together.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes alone.
Registration
The process of submitting participant details for an event.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing a particular event.
Final Round
The last competitive stage used to decide the winner.
